Task 1 - Model Selection Worksheet





Brief A - The Spiral Model (Boehm)



The spiral model would be best for the flight-control software as this model is risk-driven. Risks are explicitly assessed and resolved throughout the process. Its very Well suited to large, mission-critical projects, where errors and weak alternatives get eliminated early through explicit risk analysis. Only downside to this type of model is its very costly, complicated and the project success is very dependent on the quality of risk-analysis phase.





Brief B - Prototyping (RAD)



Prototyping would be best for the bakery website as the owner is unsure of how it should look so creating working prototypes and getting user feedback for further development would be ideal. Only downside to this is it can take long to deliver a final version based on how much needs to be changed, This overall adds the cost of the project.







Brief C - Incremental Development 



Incremental Development would be best for the university community app. It will develop an initial version of the app and expose it to customer feedback and then it can be evolved into a further versions until its complete. One issue with this type of development is there is no complete specification until the final increment which means it could take longer than expected and the was mention a slower-moving competitor.
